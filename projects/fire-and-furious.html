Fire and Furious

Overview

Fire and Furious is an action game built in Unity that combines procedural content generation with responsive AI systems. Every playthrough offers a unique experience thanks to seeded randomness, while carefully tuned enemy behaviors keep the gameplay challenging and engaging.

Screenshot: Gameplay action (1920x1080)

Combat gameplay with multiple enemy types

Procedural Level Generation

The level generation system uses seeded randomness to create consistent but varied environments. This means players can share seeds to replay specific layouts, while still enjoying the surprise of procedurally generated content.

  • Seeded RNG ensures reproducible level layouts for any given seed
  • Weighted spawning controls enemy distribution and difficulty progression
  • Modular room templates combined with procedural connections
  • Dynamic difficulty adjustment based on player progress

Design principle: Procedural generation should feel intentional, not random. Every generated level passes validation checks to ensure playability and fair challenge distribution.

Enemy AI System

Each of the 5+ enemy classes uses a finite-state-machine (FSM) architecture that makes behaviors easy to design, debug, and extend. States include idle, patrol, chase, attack, and retreat, with transitions driven by player proximity and AI health levels.

Screenshot or diagram: Enemy AI behavior (1920x1080)

FSM-based enemy AI in action

  • Clean separation between state logic and transition conditions
  • Easy to add new enemy types by combining existing states
  • Visual debugging tools to see AI state in real-time during development

Performance Optimization

Action games need to run smoothly, especially when spawning many enemies and projectiles. I implemented object pooling to eliminate garbage collection stutters and maintain stable FPS throughout intense gameplay.

  • Object pooling for enemies, projectiles, and particle effects
  • Pre-warmed pools to avoid allocation during gameplay
  • Render batching for similar sprites
  • LOD-style optimization for off-screen entities
