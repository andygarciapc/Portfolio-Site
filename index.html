Building things people actually use

I'm a software developer with a deep love for game development and systems programming. What started as modding games for fun turned into shipping real products—my mods for Mount and Blade II: Bannerlord and Worldbox have been downloaded over 20,000 times by players around the world.

I graduated from Cal State Fullerton with a B.S. in Computer Science, where I built everything from AI-powered RPGs to procedurally generated game worlds.
